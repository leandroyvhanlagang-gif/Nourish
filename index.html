How targets are estimated

Resting calories use the rounded Mifflin–St Jeor equation: 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 (male) or −161 (female). We multiply by your activity factor, then use −10% for loss or +10% for gain. Our app's simple macro split is 25% protein, 45% carbs, and 30% fat, using 4/4/9 kcal per gram. These activity factors, goal adjustments and macro split are app defaults, not part of the original equation.
